### ⚔️ Choose Your Weapon: Pro vs. Ultimate

#### ⚡ What's in the PRO Edition ($39.99)?
- **Quest Logic & Memory:** Full runtime variable engine (`SetVar`) and dynamic branching conditions (`Condition`).
- **Native TextMeshPro (TMP) Engine:** Smooth typewriter animations with sound blips and rich-text support.
- **In-Canvas Live Terminal HUD & Rewind:** Test your conversations visually on the canvas with interactive step-back / rewind (no Play Mode required).
- **RFC 4180 CSV Localization:** Export and import dialogue lines directly to Excel / Google Sheets for multi-language translation.

#### 👑 What's in the ULTIMATE Edition ($49.99)?
*Includes everything in Pro, PLUS cutting-edge AI & procedural presentation:*
- **🎙️ AI Voiceover Synthesis:** Generate NPC voices in seconds using built-in API integration with **ElevenLabs** and **OpenAI TTS** directly inside Unity.
- **🤖 Autonomous Storyteller Copilot:** Built-in narrative linter that detects broken branches, unreferenced variables, and suggests dialogue pacing improvements.
- **💬 Head-Tracking Procedural Bubble Themes:** Dynamic speech balloons anchored over character heads, thought clouds, and manga action bursts.
